Privacy Policy for Zarakia Studios Games

Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the software applications ("Games") created by Zarakia Studios.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our Games collect specific information to provide a better user experience and improve gameplay mechanics:

  • Performance Metrics: We track in-game cognitive statistics such as Agility, Vision, Accuracy, and Logic to provide player analysis.
  • Game Progress: We monitor levels reached, high scores, and Cognitive IQ results to determine player Ranks (e.g., Rank E, Rank S).
  • Device Information: Through third-party plugins like Capacitor, we may access basic device model and OS version to ensure technical compatibility.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our Games display advertisements served by Google AdMob:

  • These third-party providers may use cookies or advertising identifiers to collect information about your visit to our Games.
  • This data is used to provide personalized ads based on your interests and usage patterns.

3. Data Storage (Local Storage)

We prioritize player privacy by keeping personal progress data stored locally on your device:

  • We use localStorage to save your best IQ, high scores, and maximum level reached.
  • Your preferences, including sound settings and language selection (English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, or German), are also stored locally.
  • This data is not uploaded to our servers and remains under your exclusive control.

4. Children’s Privacy

Zarakia Studios is committed to complying with the Google Play Families Policy. Our Games do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If a Game is directed at a younger audience, we ensure that ad providers show only age-appropriate content.

5. Consent and Updates

By using our Games, you consent to this Privacy Policy. We reserve the right to update this policy as we launch new features or games. We recommend checking this page periodically for any changes.
